Solid-fill callout for briefing/AAR asides — no top border, no shadow: the tone color fills the whole box, title and body text use the lightest palette colors (paper-100 / gold-300) for contrast. Reserve this for genuinely long, multi-sentence content — don't wrap a short one-line notice in a callout box; use a plain sentence or a Badge instead.

```jsx
<Callout tone="danger" title="Rules of Engagement">Civilian casualties are strictly prohibited.</Callout>
```
