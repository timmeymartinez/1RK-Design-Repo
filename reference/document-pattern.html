Operation Silent Thunder

Situation

Enemy forces are conducting regular supply and armored movements from the port at Saint Pierre along the north-western MSR toward Montignac. The town itself is lightly held, but a rapid reaction force is stationed in Durras with an estimated response time of under ten minutes.

Mission

Echo 0 infiltrates via WHISKEY, clears the radio tower, establishes an observation post, and conducts target acquisition on enemy logistics and armored assets. Targets will be passed by grid reference to Oscar 0 for artillery engagement.

  • Engage isolated enemy only when risk of compromise is very low
  • Avoid sustained engagements
  • Civilian casualties and infrastructure damage are strictly prohibited
