OPERATION SILENT THUNDER
Mission Briefing — 1st Reforger Korps
Situation
REF: 1RK-SOC/1RK-OST-01
Enemy forces are conducting regular supply and armored movements from the port at Saint Pierre toward Montignac. A rapid reaction force is stationed in Durras, response time under ten minutes.
Rules of Engagement
  • Return fire when engaged
  • Engage isolated enemy only when risk of compromise is very low
  • Avoid sustained engagements
  • Civilian casualties and infrastructure damage are strictly prohibited
